# Annotation rules — version 1.0.0

Frozen. Do not edit this file; write a new version instead (see SKILL.md, "Versions").

First release (2026-11-06). It follows the provision model of `extract_provisions`:

- the capsule `KnowledgeStore/Capsules/coding-capsules/doc-processor/extract-provisions-spec.md`;
- the extractor prompt `ChenWeb/prompts/prompt-extract-provisions-v3.md`;
- the provision types of GB/T 1.1—2020 §9 and ISO/IEC Directives Part 2 clause 7: requirement,
  instruction, recommendation, permission, statement.

The user made three rulings for this release (openspec change `extract-provisions-benchmark`,
design D1/D2):

1. Imperative test-method steps are provisions.
2. One provision is one normative statement.
3. The first gold run is record 416.

Where the sources leave a choice open, the choice made here is marked **(benchmark rule)**, so a
later version can revisit it.

These rules must work for any document kind, language and domain: standards, regulations,
contracts, policies, specifications, procedures. The examples are Chinese standards only because
the first gold documents are.

## 1. Independence

Author the benchmark independently of the `extract_provisions` predictions:

- Do not call the processor or the provisions extract API.
- Do not read `kb.provisions` or the `.provisions` artifact until the benchmark is frozen.

Production rows are predictions, not ground truth.

Recall and precision are both required. Adding statements that are not provisions understates
the extractor's precision, just as missing provisions overstates it.

## 2. Make a full-document candidate inventory

Read every source line, including the cover, foreword, annexes, tables, notes, footnotes and
equations. For a long document, read in bounded windows and track which line IDs you have read.
Search is a second-pass aid (§4), never a replacement for reading.

Build two ledgers in the payload:

- `coverage`: non-overlapping source ranges that cover every line. Each range has review notes
  for its section, including sections that hold no provisions.
- `candidates`: every statement that could be a provision. Each candidate has:
  - a stable local ID and exact spans;
  - a disposition: `accepted`, `excluded`, `duplicate`, or temporarily `uncertain`;
  - a **`rule_id`** and a rationale;
  - the final `prov_ids`.

  Every sentence of a normative clause, every table cell with a requirement, and every annex
  step is a candidate, whether it is accepted or not.

`rule_id` must be one of the bold IDs in this file. An accepted candidate cites an `A` rule, an
excluded candidate cites an `X` rule, and a duplicate cites **D1**.

### 2.1 What a provision is

A **provision** is a statement that tells someone what must, should, may or must not be done, or
what state must hold, for the document to be applied or complied with. Statements that only
inform, define, describe, explain or point elsewhere are not provisions.

Test what the statement does, not its keywords. Ask: "Could a reader comply with this, or fail to
comply with it?" If not, it is not a provision. Some words look like modal verbs but are not: 应
inside the idiom 应收尽收, and 可 meaning "can/possible" (可降解).

### 2.2 The unit: one normative statement (user ruling)

**One provision = one normative statement.** Normally that is one sentence, or one list item or
table cell that is not a full sentence.

- **Consecutive sentences are separate provisions**, even within one numbered clause or list
  item. In Chinese, a sentence ends with 。, ！ or ？. A clause after ； stays in its sentence.
- **Split a sentence** only where:
  - its **provision type** changes (应 … 也可 … gives a mandatory and an optional provision); or
  - its **subject** (§3.3) changes ("乡镇应…；建制村应…" gives two).

  A clause without its own subject stays with the clause before it ("…应…，并及时清理…"). Several
  properties of one object stay together ("成品肥料，A 应…；B 应…" is one provision with the
  subject 成品肥料). A change of condition or object inside one subject's sentence does not split
  it either.
- **Lead-in with list items**: when a lead-in ("应符合下列要求：", "The device shall:") is followed
  by list items, each item is one provision with `location_type = heading_context`. Its text is
  the lead-in and the item joined with ` … ` (§3.2). The lead-in itself is not a separate
  provision.
- **Tables**: a table cell that states a requirement (a "要求" or "技术要求" column) is split like
  any other text. A table of values that a sentence invokes ("应达到表 2 的要求") is not split
  into provisions: the invoking sentence is the provision (X8).
- **Test methods and procedures**: each sentence of a step is one provision (A2). A step whose
  sentences are all imperatives about one action still gives one provision per sentence.

### 2.3 Accept rules

| Rule | Accept | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| **A1** | A **requirement or prohibition** stated with a mandatory marker: 应, 应当, 必须, 需, 不应, 不得, 禁止, 严禁; shall, must, is required to, shall not, must not; or the equivalent in the document's language. 应尽量 and 应尽可能 are still A1: the modal verb decides **(benchmark rule)**. | `requirement`, or `prohibition` when the statement forbids |
| **A2** | An **instruction**: an imperative sentence that tells the reader to perform an action. Covers test methods, sampling, operating and maintenance steps, and management imperatives without a subject ("建立…制度", "称取试样 3 个", "Record the temperature"). | `instruction` |
| **A3** | A **recommendation**: 宜, 不宜, 推荐, 建议, 鼓励, 提倡, 尽量 (without 应); should, should not, is recommended. | `recommendation` |
| **A4** | A **permission**: 可, 可以, 允许, 不必, 无需; may, need not, is permitted. Only when 可 grants a choice, not when it states a possibility (X7). | `permission` |
| **A5** | An **implicit requirement**: a declarative sentence in a normative clause (a requirements, management or procedure section, or a requirement column of a table) that prescribes an action, a responsible party or a state, with no marker. Examples: "可回收物运输至资源回收处理单位"; "由建制村规划建设处理站"; "运行空间环境无臭气、无污水". The section's purpose must make it binding. The same sentence in a descriptive or informative section is X4. | `requirement` (`is_explicit = false`) |

### 2.4 Exclude rules

| Rule | Exclude | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **X1** | **Statements about the document itself**: cover, publication data, foreword, drafting rules, proposer, drafters, which parts are mandatory or recommended, supersession. | "本标准按照 GB/T 1.1—2009 给出的规则起草"; "本部分第 4 章为强制性的" |
| **X2** | **Scope and applicability**: what the document covers and where it applies. | "本标准适用于农村生活垃圾分类处理" |
| **X3** | **Reference boilerplate and lists**: the normative-references lead-in, its entries, bibliography entries. | "凡是注日期的引用文件，仅所注日期的版本适用于本文件" |
| **X4** | **Definitions, classifications and descriptions**: terms and definitions, how things are classified, examples, explanations, notes that explain, how to interpret a result, what a process is. | "农村生活垃圾分为易腐垃圾、可回收物、有害垃圾和其他垃圾四大类"; "该指数若小于 100%，则表示…" |
| **X5** | **Pure pointers, headings and captions**: "见表 1", "见附录 A", a heading, a table or figure caption, table header cells, formulas. A pointer inside a provision stays in that provision. | "易腐垃圾及其他垃圾的主要处理模式见表 1。" |
| **X6** | **Objectives, principles and orientations** that prescribe no action or state a party could comply with ("以…为目标", "以…为原则", "以…为导向", "坚持…"). A sentence that names a principle and also prescribes a checkable outcome is A5 for the outcome **(benchmark rule)**. | "以减量化、资源化、无害化为目标，因地制宜…" |
| **X7** | **Statements of possibility, capability or fact**: 能, 能够, 可能, 可 meaning "can be"; can, cannot, is able to; statements of how things are. | "该设备能处理 2 t/d" |
| **X8** | **Value rows invoked by another provision**: the rows of a table of values or limits that a provision invokes ("应达到表 2 的要求"). The invoking sentence is the provision. The rows are metrics, benchmarked by `extract-metrics-benchmark`. | 表 2 "有机质的质量分数 ≥30" |

**D1** marks a duplicate of a retained provision: the same statement found twice during
inventory (overlapping windows), or a fragment of a sentence already accepted whole.

A candidate that fits no rule is never accepted for that reason. Keep it `uncertain` and resolve
it before saving. If it shows a gap in these rules, report it in `limitations` and do not save.

## 3. Label each accepted provision

### 3.1 Kind and type

`provision_kind` is one of `requirement`, `prohibition`, `instruction`, `recommendation`,
`permission`. `provision_type` follows from it. `validate` enforces the mapping:

| `provision_kind` | `provision_type` |
|---|---|
| `requirement`, `prohibition`, `instruction` | `mandatory` |
| `recommendation` | `recommended` |
| `permission` | `optional` |

A sentence that both requires and forbids with the same subject ("应放在冰箱保存，但保存时间不应超过
48 h") keeps one row. Use `requirement` unless the whole statement is a ban.

### 3.2 Text, location and evidence

- `provision`: the statement **exactly as written**, without its clause number ("7.1.1 ") and
  without list dashes. It may keep or drop the final 。.
  - Non-contiguous parts (a lead-in and its item, or text interrupted by a page-break line) are
    joined with ` … `.
  - Each part must occur in the cited lines joined in line order.
  - For a table cell, the text is the cell content without HTML tags.
- `provision_en`: an English translation for non-English documents (may be `""`), `""` for
  English documents. Not scored.
- `section`: the heading of the section the provision is in, as written (number and title). For
  an annex, use the annex heading and its subclause ("附录 B 植物种子发芽试验 / B.1 …").
- `source_line_spans`: `"N"` or `"N-M"` strings, minimal, sorted, merged. Cite every line the
  provision's text comes from, including a lead-in line.
- `source_table_rows`: for a provision inside an HTML table line, `[{"line": N, "rows": ["r2"]}]`,
  with IDs as `ParseTableGrid` assigns them: header rows `h0…`, data rows `r1, r2, …`. For
  rowspans, cite the row the cell starts in. Otherwise `[]`.
- `evidence`: exact substrings of the cited lines `[{"line": N, "quote": "…"}]`, covering the
  statement.

### 3.3 Subject and name

- `provision_subject`: who or what the provision binds, that is, the party that must act or the
  object that must satisfy the statement.
  - Normally this is the grammatical subject ("建制村", "垃圾分类收集容器", "收运过程").
  - For a property statement ("X 的 Y 应…", "X，Y 应…"), it is the object X that owns the
    property, not the property Y.
  - For instructions and subjectless sentences, use the object the action applies to ("新鲜物料试样",
    "浸出液").
  - For `heading_context` items, use the subject of the lead-in.
  - Write it as the text writes it, without articles or modifiers that are not in the text.
- `provision_subject_en`: required for non-English documents; `null` for English documents.
- `prov_name`: a short name of what the provision regulates, in the document's language
  ("分类收集容器有盖", "浸出液保存期限"). `prov_name_en` is required for non-English documents and
  `null` for English documents. Not scored; used to find rows.

### 3.4 Explicitness

`is_explicit` is `true` when the statement carries its own normative marker. Markers are an
A1/A3/A4 word, or the imperative mood of an A2 instruction. `heading_context` items take the
lead-in's marker and count as explicit. `is_explicit` is `false` for A5.

### 3.5 Location type

`location_type` is decided from the cited unit and the parser's line types:

| Value | When |
|---|---|
| `bullet` | the provision is a whole `list-item` line (or a list item wrapped onto the next line). Includes numbered clauses the parser types as list items |
| `paragraph` | the provision is a whole `paragraph` line |
| `sentence` | the provision is part of a paragraph or list item: one of several sentences, or part of a split sentence |
| `table_cell` | the provision is inside one table cell. Use it for any provision in a requirement column |
| `table_row` | the provision spans several cells of one row |
| `heading_context` | a list item that takes its modal verb or subject from a lead-in (§2.2) |
| `mixed` | anything else: several paragraphs, or a paragraph plus a table |

`validate` refuses `table_row`/`table_cell` on non-table lines, and every other value on table
lines.

### 3.6 Confidence

`confidence` is a finite number from 0 to 1 for the reviewer's certainty. It never replaces
review.

## 4. Audit completeness, correctness and consistency separately

1. **Completeness.** Re-read each section without relying on the drafted rows. Then, as a second
   pass, search for:
   - every marker of §2.3 (应 不应 不得 禁止 严禁 必须 需 宜 不宜 鼓励 提倡 尽量 可 可以 允许 不必;
     shall must should may);
   - imperatives at sentence start in procedures;
   - declarative sentences in requirement sections (A5).

   Reconcile every hit with a row or an explained exclusion. Check that:
   - every sentence of every normative clause is accounted for;
   - sentences were split where type or subject changes (§2.2);
   - requirement cells of tables were read;
   - annex procedures gave one row per sentence.
2. **Correctness.** Verify every field against the cited lines:
   - text as written, with no paraphrase;
   - kind by the marker, and type by the mapping;
   - `is_explicit`;
   - subject by §3.3;
   - location type by §3.5;
   - spans and table rows.
3. **Consistency.**
   - The same kind of statement gets the same labels. Every "X 由 Y 负责" and every
     "X 运输至 Y" in requirement sections is A5.
   - No excluded candidate (X4, X6, X7) is accepted elsewhere for the same wording, and the
     reverse.
   - Every 可 is either A4 or X7, decided by meaning.

   Re-decide both sides of a conflict. Do not edit rationales to fit.

Write concrete checks and findings in `completeness_review` and `correctness_review`. Put the
consistency findings in `correctness_review` under a "Consistency:" heading. Resolve every
uncertain candidate; never relabel uncertainty as exclusion to pass validation. If parser damage,
missing context or a rules gap remains, keep the draft, name the problem in `limitations`, and do
not save.
