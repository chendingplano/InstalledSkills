# Annotation rules — version 1.0.0

Frozen. Do not edit this file; write a new version instead (see SKILL.md, "Versions").

First release (2026-11-08). It follows the product model of `extract_products`:

- the capsule `KnowledgeStore/Capsules/coding-capsules/doc-processor/extract-products-spec.md`,
  including its producthood gate ("Pass 1 is the pipeline's producthood gate");
- the extractor prompts `ChenWeb/prompts/prompt-extract-product-mentions-v2.md`,
  `prompt-enrich-product-mention-v4.md` and `prompt-extract-products-merged-v2.md`. Their
  vocabularies are the stored long names used here.

The user made four rulings for this release (openspec change `extract-products-benchmark`,
design D1–D3):

1. One gold row is one (product, relation_type) pair, as in `kb.products`. Rows of one product
   share a `product_key`.
2. Waste categories and waste streams are products.
3. Besides name, type, relation and evidence, every row carries obligation level, requirement
   text (exact source text), conditions, exceptions, parameters, related products and
   responsible actor, and all of them are graded.
4. The first gold run is record 416.

Where the sources leave a choice open, the choice made here is marked **(benchmark rule)**, so a
later version can revisit it.

These rules must work for any document kind, language and domain: product standards, test
methods, regulations, management standards, specifications, catalogues, papers. The examples are
Chinese standards only because the first gold documents are.

## 1. Independence

Author the benchmark independently of the `extract_products` predictions:

- Do not call the processor or the products extract API.
- Do not read `kb.products`, `kb.product_names` or the `.products` artifact until the benchmark
  is frozen.

Production rows are predictions, not ground truth. If you saw production rows before starting
(for example while preparing the run), say so in `correctness_review`, and decide every row from
the source alone.

Recall and precision are both required. Accepting things that are not products understates the
extractor's precision; dropping real products overstates it. "Dropping a real product is as much
an error as extracting a facility" (extractor Rule 7).

## 2. Make a full-document candidate inventory

Read every source line, including the cover, foreword, definitions, example lists, annexes,
tables, notes and equations. For a long document, read in bounded windows and track which line
IDs you have read. Search is a second-pass aid (§4), never a replacement for reading.

Build two ledgers in the payload:

- `coverage`: non-overlapping line ranges that cover every line. Each range has review notes for
  its section, including sections that name no products.
- `candidates`: every noun phrase that could name a product. A candidate is one distinct surface
  form. Its spans cite where it occurs; repeated occurrences of the same surface are one
  candidate. Each candidate has:
  - a stable local ID, its `text` exactly as written, and its spans;
  - a disposition: `accepted`, `excluded`, `duplicate`, or temporarily `uncertain`;
  - a **`rule_id`** and a rationale;
  - `product_key`: the gold product it became (accepted) or repeats (duplicate); empty for
    excluded candidates.

  Every concrete noun phrase naming an object, device, container, vehicle, material, substance,
  waste category, facility, organization or measured constituent is a candidate, whether it is
  accepted or not. Abstract nouns that obviously name no thing (管理, 分类, 要求) need not be
  listed.

`rule_id` must be one of the bold IDs in this file. An accepted candidate cites an `A` rule, an
excluded candidate an `X` rule, and a duplicate **D1**. Every gold row cites the `R` rule that
decided its relation type.

### 2.1 What a product is

A **product** is a tangible or software object that can be designed, manufactured, sold, bought,
supplied, installed, used, tested, maintained, regulated, certified, inspected, recalled or
disposed of. The test for a physical thing: is it supplied as a movable article ("you could put
it on a truck"), or is it built or established at a site? Only the first is a product. A product
stays a product when it is discarded: 废旧家具 and 易拉罐 in a waste-sorting list are products.

### 2.2 Accept rules

| Rule | Accept | Typical `product_type` |
|---|---|---|
| **A1** | A **manufactured article**: devices, apparatus, instruments, machines, vehicles, containers and bins, tools, appliances, household articles, printed matter, components, accessories, packaging and consumables, new or discarded. Includes articles named only as a means or tool of an activity ("通过发放图册", "置于聚乙烯瓶中"). | `equipment`, `specific_product`, `component`, `packaging`, `consumable` |
| **A2** | A **product class**: a category term that groups several kinds of products ("电器电子产品", "包装物", "废弃纸制品", "medical devices"). | `product_class` |
| **A3** | A **waste category or waste stream** (user ruling). Waste categories ("可回收物", "易腐垃圾", "有害垃圾", "其他垃圾", "餐厨垃圾", "大件垃圾", "农村生活垃圾") and solid or liquid waste streams that are collected, transported, treated or disposed of ("渗滤液", "废水", "废渣", "飞灰", "炉渣", "沼渣", "沼液"). | `product_class`, or `material` for a stream named by its material ("废玻璃", "渗滤液") |
| **A4** | A **material or substance** treated as a good, an input or a test material: raw materials, chemicals and reagents, water used as a reagent, fuels, fertilizers, biological materials (seeds, microbial strains) and process outputs ("沼气", "成品肥料", "浸出液"). | `material`, `consumable` |
| **A5** | A **software product**: software or an information system supplied as a licensed product. | `software`, `system` |

### 2.3 Exclude rules

| Rule | Exclude | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **X1** | **Places, facilities and works**: anything built or established at a site, and locations. Facilities, plants, stations, depots, collection or storage points, centres, bases, buildings, rooms, pits and tanks built in place, civil works and projects, on-site systems and pipe networks; public places and regions. Chinese cues: a name ending in 站, 厂, 场, 基地, 中心, 园, 区, 房, 池, 工程, 设施, or 点 in the locational sense. A 系统 is a product only when supplied as a product (A5); a physical system assembled on site is X1. | "垃圾转运站", "生活垃圾焚烧厂", "有害垃圾独立贮存点", "堆肥设施（阳光房）", "沼气工程", "农村户用沼气池", "污水收集和处理设施", "管网", "宣传栏", "公共场所" |
| **X2** | **Citation-only mentions.** A noun phrase whose only support is a normative-reference entry, a bibliography entry or the title of a cited document. A citation line is never evidence. | **X3** | **Parties**: organizations, units, people, roles, administrative units, households. | "建制村", "生活垃圾收运单位", "村民", "集中供餐单位" |
| **X4** | **Non-things**: abstract concepts, technologies and methods, processes, activities and services, rules, systems of management, records and documents (台账, 制度, 预案, 规程), legal acts, document sections, and marks or symbols. | "物联网", "堆肥", "无害化处理", "管理台账", "垃圾分类标志" |
| **X5** | **Properties, quantities and emissions**: a property, parameter or measured constituent of a product, and pollutants or emissions named as something discharged rather than handled. | **X6** | **References and placeholders** that name no product of their own: anaphora, generic heads, sample roles, and quantified collectives. | "该设备", "各类垃圾", "新鲜物料试样", "样品", "固体废弃物" used as the genus of a definition |

**D1** marks a candidate that repeats an accepted product:
- the same surface found twice (overlapping windows);
- a variant that differs only by normalization (full/half width, spacing, punctuation);
- a name the document explicitly equates with it ("（以下简称…）", "又称…", "i.e.").

Two different names are two products otherwise, even when they seem to mean the same thing
("成品肥料" and "肥料"; "分类垃圾容器" and "垃圾分类收集容器"). The gold set under-merges on
purpose, as product-name resolution does **(benchmark rule)**.

A candidate that fits no rule is never accepted for that reason. Keep it `uncertain` and resolve
it before saving. If it shows a gap in these rules, report it in `limitations` and do not save.

### 2.4 Names

- `product_name`: the product's noun phrase **as written**. Drop leading determiners, quantities,
  and evaluative or requirement adjectives ("各种", "各类", "相应的", "干净的", "密闭的", "500 ml
  具密封塞"). Keep the modifiers that make it a product kind ("废", "旧", "分类", "有害垃圾收集").
  A name broken across two lines is joined ("电话" + "机" = "电话机"). `validate` checks that
  the name occurs in the cited lines (whitespace-insensitive).
- **Coordinations with an elided shared head** ("收集、运输设备", "水堇或萝卜种子",
  "废弃药品及其包装物") give one product per conjunct. A conjunct that is not written out whole
  ("收集设备", "水堇种子", "废弃药品包装物") is written expanded, with
  `name_expanded: true` and `name_source` set to the exact source phrase it comes from. A conjunct
  written whole keeps its own name ("运输设备", "萝卜种子", "废弃药品").
- `canonical_name`: the normalized base name, used to recognise the same product across
  documents. Drop waste-status words ("废", "废弃", "废旧", "旧") and document-local qualifiers,
  keep the product kind: "废荧光灯管" → "荧光灯管", "废旧家具" → "家具". Waste categories and
  streams (A3) keep their own name ("可回收物", "废玻璃"). In most other cases
  `canonical_name = product_name`.
- `product_name_en`, `canonical_name_en`: English names, required for non-English documents,
  `null` for English documents.

All rows of one `product_key` have the same `product_name`, `canonical_name` and `product_type`.

### 2.5 Product type

Decide `product_type` in this order (first match):

1. `software`: software and licensed information systems.
2. `system`: an integrated system supplied as one product.
3. `equipment`: machines, devices, apparatus, instruments, vehicles, and containers or bins used
   as equipment ("垃圾桶", "收集容器", "培养箱", "往复式水平震荡机").
4. `component`: a part or accessory named as part of another product.
5. `packaging`: containers and wrappings that package goods ("包装物", "牛奶饮料纸包装",
   "用于包装的桶、箱、瓶").
6. `consumable`: items used up in use ("滤纸", "滤膜", "餐巾纸", "卫生巾").
7. `material`: substances, chemicals, reagents, fuels, fertilizers, biological materials, and
   material streams ("去离子水", "沼气", "成品肥料", "废玻璃", "渗滤液").
8. `product_class`: a category that groups several kinds of products, including waste
   categories ("可回收物", "电器电子产品", "有害垃圾").
9. `specific_product`: any other single kind of article ("易拉罐", "报纸", "电视机", "废荧光灯管").
10. `other`: only when nothing above fits.

`unknown` is never a gold value.

## 3. Relations: one row per supported relation type

### 3.1 The unit (user ruling)

One gold row is one (product, `relation_type`) pair. A product appears once per document, so it
has at most one row per relation type. That row cites **every** statement in the document that
supports the relation, wherever it is.

Include a relation type only when the evidence directly and clearly supports it. Do not add
marginal or inferred relations (extractor Relation Rule 3). Every accepted product has at least
one row: a product with no specific supported relation gets exactly one `scope` row (R7).

### 3.2 Relation rules

Each statement about a product supports one relation type. When one statement fits several,
use the first that fits in this order: R3, R4, R1, R2, R5, R6, R7. Different statements can
support different relation types; each type gives its own row.

| Rule | `relation_type` | When |
|---|---|---|
| **R1** | a specific requirement type (table below) | A statement requires something of the product, and its subject matter fits one specific type. |
| **R2** | `requirement_target` | A statement requires something of the product that fits no R1 type. This includes duties to provide or equip the product ("应配备分类垃圾桶"), and treatment and disposal duties for wastes ("应委托…进行无害化处置") **(benchmark rule)**. A property demanded inside a provide/equip duty ("配备密闭的垃圾转运车辆") stays in this row's `requirement_text`; it does not add a design row. |
| **R3** | `prohibited_product` / `exempted_product` | The product is prohibited, banned or restricted; or it is expressly excluded or exempted from the document, a definition or a requirement ("不包括…建筑垃圾、医疗垃圾"). |
| **R4** | `component_of` / `contains_product` / `compatible_with` / `replacement_or_alternative` | The document states a part–whole, containment, compatibility or substitution link between two products. Class membership and examples ("易拉罐等废金属物") are not R4; they are R7. `related_products` names the other product. |
| **R5** | `measurement_object` / `risk_source` | The product is measured, monitored, detected or tested as the object of a measurement (a test sample's material, an extract that is analysed); or it creates, contributes to or controls a hazard. |
| **R6** | `regulated_object` | The document places the product under a legal or compliance regime as such: a licence, permit, registration or hazardous-material regime ("委托具有相应危险废物经营许可证的单位"). A requirement that only cites a standard ("应符合 CJJ 52 的要求") is not R6; it is the R1/R2 relation of its subject matter. |
| **R7** | `scope` | The product is within the document's subject matter without a requirement: scope clauses, definitions, classifications and example lists, applicability descriptions, and the one fallback row of §3.1. |
| **R8** | `other` | Only when a supported relation fits none of the above. |

R1 subject matter:

| `relation_type` | The requirement is about |
|---|---|
| `performance_requirement` | performance, quality, safety, reliability, function, operating state, or limits on the product's own properties |
| `design_requirement` | how it is designed, configured, structured or constructed (has a lid, is fully enclosed) |
| `material_requirement` | materials or substances it must use, avoid or limit (heavy-metal limits, permitted ingredients) |
| `testing_requirement` | testing, inspection, calibration, verification or validation of the product, including a normative test method whose test object is the product |
| `certification_requirement` | certification, approval, registration, conformity certificates, labelling and marking ("应有产品合格证", "应印制垃圾分类标志") |
| `usage_condition` | how, where or under which conditions the product is used or operated ("盖好垃圾桶", "应放在冰箱保存" for the refrigerator) |
| `installation_requirement` | installation, mounting, connection, commissioning |
| `maintenance_requirement` | maintenance, servicing, repair, cleaning, replacement, periodic checks |
| `storage_requirement` | storage, transport, preservation, handling. For wastes and discarded articles this includes depositing, separate collection and transport ("应单独投放", "每日定时收运", "运输至资源回收处理单位") **(benchmark rule)** |

### 3.3 Row fields

- `product_rel_id`: `<record_id>_gprd_<n>`, numbered in document order of first evidence.
- `product_key`: `<record_id>_gp_<n>`, numbered in document order of first mention.
- `relation_summary`: one concise sentence, in the document's language, on how the document
  relates to the product through this relation. `relation_summary_en` is optional. Not graded.
- `evidence_lines`: every line that supports this relation, as production spans: `"N"`,
  `"N-M"` for consecutive lines, and table rows `"N#rK"` / `"N#hK"` with row IDs as
  `ParseTableGrid` assigns them (header rows `h0…`, data rows `r1, r2, …`; a rowspan cell
  belongs to the row it starts in). A table line is always cited by rows, never as `"N"`
  **(benchmark rule)**. Never cite a citation line (X2).
- `evidence_quote`: one exact substring of one cited line (or of one cited row's cell text) that
  best shows the relation.
- `obligation_level`, per statement:

  | Value | When |
  |---|---|
  | `prohibited` | a prohibition (不得, 禁止, 严禁, shall not), and every `prohibited_product` row |
  | `mandatory` | 应, 应当, 必须, 需, shall, must; and a declarative statement in a normative clause that prescribes an action or state without a marker ("可回收物运输至资源回收处理单位") |
  | `conditional` | the whole obligation is triggered by a stated condition ("采用非垃圾压缩车直接清运方式的，应…"); the condition goes in `conditions` |
  | `recommended` | 宜, 鼓励, 提倡, should |
  | `permitted` | 可, 可以, 允许, may |
  | `descriptive` | no duty: scope, definitions, classifications, examples, measurement objects, exemptions |

  When several statements support one row, use the strongest: prohibited > mandatory >
  conditional > recommended > permitted > descriptive. `unknown` is never a gold value.
- `requirement_text`: the clause that states the requirement, **as written**, including its modal
  verb ("垃圾分类收集容器应有盖"). Several statements, or a clause interrupted by other text, are
  joined with ` … `; each part must occur in the cited lines. `""` for a `descriptive` row.
  `requirement_text_en` is optional. Not a paraphrase: the user's ruling grades it by similarity
  to this text.
- `conditions`: short source-faithful phrases that restrict when the requirement applies ("发现破
  旧、污损或者数量不足时", "污水收集后纳入管网的"). `exceptions`: phrases that carve something
  out of it ("但保存时间不应超过 48 h" is a limit, not an exception; "除…以外" is). `[]` when
  none.
- `parameters`: `{"name", "value", "unit"}` for each quantity the evidence of this row states for
  the product. `value` keeps the source comparator and number ("≥30", "5.5~8.5", "2");
  `unit` is the unit as written or `null`; `name` is the quantity's name as written (the table
  row label). `[]` when none.
- `related_products`: `[{"product_name", "relationship"}]`, non-empty exactly for the four R4
  types; `relationship` is `component_of contains_product compatible_with
  replacement_or_alternative compared_with other`.
- `responsible_actor`: the party the source names as obliged to fulfil the requirement, as
  written ("建制村", "生活垃圾收运单位"); `""` when none is named or the row is descriptive.
- `section`: the heading of the section of the first evidence line, as written.
- `confidence`: a finite number from 0 to 1 for the reviewer's certainty. It never replaces
  review.

## 4. Audit completeness, correctness and consistency separately

1. **Completeness.** Re-read each section without relying on the drafted rows. Then, as a second
   pass, search for:
   - article and equipment heads (桶 箱 袋 瓶 罐 筐 车 车辆 设备 装置 器 机 仪 计 容器 包装 剂 肥
     料 纸 管 电池; device apparatus container vehicle);
   - waste words (垃圾 废 废弃 废旧 旧 渣 液 物);
   - every item of every example list and every table cell;
   - every requirement sentence (应 不得 宜 可; shall should may), to check each product named in
     it has the relation the sentence supports.

   Reconcile every hit with a row or an explained exclusion. Check that every candidate has a
   decision, and every accepted product has all and only its supported relation types.
2. **Correctness.** Verify every field against the cited lines:
   - names as written, expansions marked;
   - type by §2.5;
   - relation by §3.2, obligation by its marker;
   - requirement text as written, with no paraphrase;
   - conditions, exceptions, parameters (value and unit as written), related products and actor;
   - spans and table rows.
3. **Consistency.**
   - The same kind of thing gets the same decision: every facility is X1, every 收集容器 is a
     product, every measured constituent is X5.
   - The same kind of statement gets the same relation: every deposit or transport duty for a
     waste is `storage_requirement`, every treatment duty is `requirement_target`.
   - No product has two rows of one relation type.
   - No excluded surface is accepted elsewhere, and the reverse.

   Re-decide both sides of a conflict. Do not edit rationales to fit.

Write concrete checks and findings in `completeness_review` and `correctness_review`. Put the
consistency findings in `correctness_review` under a "Consistency:" heading. Resolve every
uncertain candidate; never relabel uncertainty as exclusion to pass validation. If parser damage,
missing context or a rules gap remains, keep the draft, name the problem in `limitations`, and do
not save.
